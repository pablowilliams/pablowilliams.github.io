MSc Business Analytics at University College London with a focus on artificial intelligence and natural language processing. Graduated Upper Second Class from Lancaster University in BSc Business Analytics.

Currently an Applied AI Engineering Intern at QuantiFire, building IRIS - an AI investor-relations agent that answers questions across filings, surveys and shareholder data, with a verifier council scoring every claim. Previously a Data Analyst at APJ Kapital, turning filings, earnings calls and market news into buy, hold and sell calls. Earlier at MDOTM in Milan, where the AI investment tools I helped build contributed to an AIFIN Financial Innovation Award winning platform.

Trilingual in Italian and English, fluent in Spanish, learning German. Most happy when the model, the data and the business question finally line up.

From data to deployment. I care about end to end pipelines. Ingesting raw sources, cleaning and enriching features, training and calibrating models, then wrapping the result in an interface a non technical stakeholder can actually use.

I am not dogmatic about the stack. Python and PyTorch are home, but I reach for React, Three.js or FastAPI the moment an idea needs a live interface.

Happy to chat about quant finance, interpretability, calibration, or how to get a transformer to run inside a browser.
