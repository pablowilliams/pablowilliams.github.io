## The question

Can a busy interviewer move from a concise account of my experience to working projects, source code and research evidence without an artificial landing gate or hidden navigation?

## What I built

- A static, responsive portfolio that groups applied AI work, quantitative research, technical explainers, experience and credentials in one accessible document.
- Progressive interactions for filtering, section navigation and project discovery, while keeping the underlying links and copy readable without JavaScript.
- A deterministic inventory that measures the committed page and creates `reports/results.json` and an SVG from the same source.
- Unit and end-to-end quality gates covering document integrity, browser behavior, mobile overflow, reduced motion, contact paths and downloadable evidence.

## What I found

The 16 September 2026 inventory records 14 sections, 49 project or demo cards, 29 research cards, 100 GitHub links and no missing internal targets or duplicate IDs. These are structural counts, not claims about the quality or availability of every linked repository.

## Skills evidenced

Semantic HTML; responsive CSS; JavaScript; accessibility; structured metadata; static deployment; browser testing; CI/CD; Docker; reproducible reporting; technical writing.

## Why it matters

The page is the routing layer for the wider portfolio. Removing two cinematic intro systems means a recruiter reaches the work immediately, keyboard users are no longer trapped behind a start control, and automated checks can inspect the same content a visitor receives.

## Limitations and next steps

- External demos can move or become unavailable independently of this repository.
- The current single document is large; the next design phase should split long-form collections into static detail pages and remove unused legacy media.
- Per-project quantitative claims must continue to be verified in their source repositories rather than inferred from this index.
